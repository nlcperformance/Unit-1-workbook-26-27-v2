Learner Profile

Learning Aim A: The Skeletal System

Anatomical Structure

Reference diagram of the Axial and Appendicular skeleton.

S.R.A.P. Physiological Chain

Structure

Synovial Joint Cavity & Membrane.

Response

Secretes thinner synovial fluid, lubricating the joint space.

Adaptation

Ligaments stretch and become more pliable over time.

Performance

Increased flexibility allows a larger gymnastics split leap.

Synoptic Exam Task (6 Marks)

Analyse how a 6-month resistance training programme impacts Bone Mineral Density (BMD) and ligament strength in a basketball player.

Learning Aim B: The Muscular System

S.R.A.P. Physiological Chain

Structure

Type IIx Fast-Glycolytic Fibres.

Response

Suffer micro-tears during heavy eccentric muscle contractions.

Adaptation

Fibres repair thicker, increasing protein size (Hypertrophy).

Performance

Generates explosive power for a 100m sprint start.

Synoptic Exam Task (6 Marks)

Assess how the "All-or-None Law" governs motor unit recruitment when an 800m runner shifts pace into a 100m sprint finish.

Learning Aim C: The Respiratory System

Anatomical Structure

Reference diagram of the Lungs and Alveoli conducting zone.

S.R.A.P. Physiological Chain

Structure

Alveolar Capillary Network.

Response

Capillaries expand to increase rapid gaseous exchange diffusion.

Adaptation

Capillarisation increases surface area around the alveoli.

Performance

Maximises VO2 Max for elite marathon running endurance.

Synoptic Exam Task (8 Marks)

Evaluate how long-term altitude training ($\text{above } 2400\,\text{m}$) triggers physiological adaptations in the respiratory system to enhance performance at sea level.

Learning Aim D: Cardiovascular System

Anatomical Structure

Reference diagram of the internal structure of the Heart.

S.R.A.P. Physiological Chain

Structure

Left Ventricle Wall.

Response

Heart rate increases to pump oxygenated blood faster.

Adaptation

Cardiac Hypertrophy increases maximum stroke volume output.

Performance

Delays fatigue during the final sprint in cycling.

Synoptic Exam Task (6 Marks)

Analyse how the sympathetic and parasympathetic nervous systems regulate the cardiac cycle before, during, and after a high-intensity interval training (HIIT) session.
